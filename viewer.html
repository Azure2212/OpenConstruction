In-browser viewer beta

Renders AEC formats right in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. Drag a file onto the stage or use “Open file”. Supported: .xyz / .pts / .ply (point clouds) and .ifc (BIM, experimental).

Runs entirely client-side with WebGL — $0 hosting, no data leaves the browser. This is the “close the last mile” preview: a contributor can ship a viewable asset URL and the catalog renders it here instead of sending users elsewhere. IFC support is experimental and loads the open-source web-ifc engine on demand.
